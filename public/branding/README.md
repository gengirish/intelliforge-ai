# IntelliForge AI — sponsor & brand asset pack

Everything an event organiser, partner or press contact needs. All files in this
folder are public and served from the site, so you can send a link instead of
attachments:

**https://www.intelliforge.tech/branding/** + the filename below.

Generated by `python scripts/build-brand-assets.py` from the canonical palette
in `app/globals.css` and the navbar wordmark — re-run it after a brand change
rather than hand-editing these files. The interim site mark (`public/logo.svg`,
`app/icon.tsx`) is unchanged; see `docs/branding-assets.md` for the remaining
designer checklist.

## Usage rules

- Use the **horizontal lockup** wherever the company name has to be readable.
  The square mark alone is only for sponsor grids, avatars and favicons.
- Pick the variant that matches the background: `light-bg` files have dark text
  and must sit on white or a light colour; `dark-bg` files have white text.
- Keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of the square mark's
  corner radius (about ¼ of the mark's height) on all sides.
- Don't recolour, stretch, rotate, add effects to, or place the logo on a busy
  photo. Scale proportionally only.
- Minimum size: 24px tall for the square mark, 120px wide for the lockup.
